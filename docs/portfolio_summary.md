# Portfolio summary

Reconstructed a dimensioned 27-body 1U CubeSat in SolidWorks and simulated periodic conduction and radiation in ANSYS MAPDL. Completed mesh, time-step and radiation-resolution studies; audited energy conservation and periodicity; evaluated six assumed input ranges using 12 endpoint simulations and two nonlinear joint checks.

The refined case has pooled component-average RMSE 4.09 K against published curves. This is a verified numerical reconstruction with disclosed source discrepancies, rather than experimental or flight validation. Assumed uncertainty ranges are engineering scenarios, not measured confidence intervals.

See the [full report](../results/Numerical_Uncertainty_Report.md) and [reproduction instructions](reproduction.md).
